TYPES OF HAIRSTYLE

  • Ponytail-School,office or casual look
    • ✅ Face ko clean or smart look deti hai.
    ✅ Summer me specially cool lagti hai.
    ✅ Har outfit par suit hoti hai.
    ✅ Short time me ready ho jati hai.

  • Braids (choti)- Stylish or neat look
    • ✅ Stylish hairstyle
    ✅ Simple look ko stylish banata hai
    ✅ Har outfit par perfect
    ✅ Long-lasting look

  • Bun hairstyle-Shadi,party or traditional look
    • ✅ Rounded shape me bandha jata hai
    ✅ Summer me perfect
    ✅ Clean & stylish look

  • Curls & Waves-Glamorous look
    • Curl waves hairstyle ek soft, romantic aur stylish hairstyle hai.
