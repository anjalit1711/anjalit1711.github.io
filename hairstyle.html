TYPES OF HAIRSTYLE

  • Ponytail - School, office or casual look

    • ✅ Face ko clean aur smart look deti hai.
    ✅ Summer me cool lagti hai.
    ✅ Har outfit par suit hoti hai.
    ✅ Jaldi ready ho jati hai.

  • Braids (Choti) - Stylish and neat look

    • ✅ Stylish hairstyle
    ✅ Simple look ko stylish banata hai
    ✅ Har outfit par perfect
    ✅ Long-lasting look

  • Bun Hairstyle - Wedding & traditional look

    • ✅ Rounded shape me bandha jata hai
    ✅ Summer ke liye perfect
    ✅ Clean aur stylish look deta hai

  • Curls & Waves - Glamorous look

    • Soft aur romantic hairstyle hai.
    Hair ko volume aur bounce deta hai,
    jisse look attractive ho jata hai.

🌺 WHY HAIRSTYLE IS IMPORTANT 🌺

1. Hairstyle brings out your best features.
2. Matches your personality and fashion.
3. Improves overall look.
4. Face shape ko enhance karta hai.
5. First impression strong banata hai.
